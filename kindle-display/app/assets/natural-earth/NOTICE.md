# Land data

Natural Earth 1:110m land, version tag v5.1.2.
Downloaded 2026-09-05 from the maintainer's repository:
https://raw.githubusercontent.com/nvkelso/natural-earth-vector/v5.1.2/geojson/ne_110m_land.geojson

Public domain: https://www.naturalearthdata.com/about/terms-of-use/
No country borders, Apple assets or TRMNL source are included.

The renderer samples these polygons onto its own regular dot grid and uses a
Miller cylindrical projection cropped to 83 N / 60 S. Polar areas outside that
crop are intentionally not shown; the solar mask itself is spherical.

Astronomy reference (approximate recreational display, not navigation):
https://www.gml.noaa.gov/grad/solcalc/solareqns.PDF
The glass highlight is a decorative material cue, not a twilight measurement.
